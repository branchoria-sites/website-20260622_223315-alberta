## Introduction

One of the clearest recent examples of an ordinary explanation for an apparent UFO over [Quebec]({{ 'what-really-happened-in-quebecs-ufo/' | relative_url }}) came from Montreal in March 2023. ## Why formation lights distort distance and size

Night-time observations remove many of the visual clues that people normally use to judge what they are seeing. Aircraft bodies disappear into darkness, leaving only navigation lights, landing lights or other illuminated points visible.

When helicopters fly in a tight formation, several effects can make them resemble a single triangular object:

* **Loss of scale:** Without a visible aircraft body, observers cannot easily estimate distance. Nearby helicopters may appear to be one enormous object farther away.
* **Geometric patterns:** Three or more aircraft can create stable triangular arrangements that resemble the classic "black triangle" UFO description.
* **Limited depth perception:** At night, separate aircraft can appear to occupy the same plane in space even when they are separated by considerable distances.
* **Apparent silence:** Sound does not always travel as expected in urban environments. Traffic, buildings and atmospheric conditions can reduce or delay rotor noise.
* **Slow movement:** Helicopters can hover, move slowly or change direction in ways that differ from the public's expectations of conventional aircraft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.newsweek.com/ufo-montreal-video-twitter-identified-1787925" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: newsweek.com">[Newsweek]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">newsweek.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">More. ## Why triangular UFO reports often involve aircraft

The triangular UFO is one of the most persistent modern sighting patterns. Yet many reports involve observations made at night where only lights are visible. In those circumstances, the human brain naturally tries to connect separate points of light into a single shape.

Military aviation can contribute to this effect because formation flying deliberately places aircraft in visually organised patterns. ## How to check aircraft before calling it unresolved

The Montreal case offers a practical lesson for anyone who sees unusual lights over Quebec.

Before treating a sighting as genuinely unexplained, it is useful to check:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--step-flow" markdown="1">

1. **Flight-tracking services** to see whether civilian aircraft were operating in the area.
2. **Military activity** that may not appear on public tracking systems.
3. **The number of visible lights**, asking whether they could belong to multiple aircraft rather than one object.
4. **Video zoom and perspective effects**, which can compress distances and merge separate lights into a single pattern.
5. **Whether the sighting occurred near airports, military facilities or known training routes.**

</div>

A sighting should be considered unresolved only after ordinary possibilities have been examined and found wanting. ## What this case adds to Quebec's UFO history

Quebec's UFO record contains a mixture of unresolved reports, misidentifications and cases that remain debated decades later. The March 2023 Montreal formation video belongs firmly in the category of a likely aviation explanation. Its value lies not in mystery but in illustration.
