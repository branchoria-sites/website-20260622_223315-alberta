Carman is an agricultural community in [Manitoba]({{ 'why-manitoba-matters-in-canadian-ufo/' | relative_url }})’s Pembina Valley region, southwest of Winnipeg. In the mid-1970s, the area consisted largely of farms, open prairie, small towns, and relatively dark night skies. The region’s sparse population and broad horizons made unusual aerial lights especially noticeable.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carman%2C_Manitoba" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Carman, Manitoba</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Carman, Manitoba</span></span></span>

Before the [Charlie Red Star]({{ 'charlie-red-star/' | relative_url }}) wave became famous, Manitoba already possessed a notable place in Canadian UFO lore because of the 1967 [Falcon Lake]({{ 'falcon-lake/' | relative_url }}) incident. Yet the Carman events differed in a significant way. Rather than a single dramatic encounter, they developed into a prolonged series of recurring observations involving many independent witnesses over an extended period. Researchers later classified the events as a UFO “flap,” meaning a localized concentration of sightings occurring within a relatively short timeframe.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://darkpoutine.com/2023/10/289-spooktober-3-more-on-canadian-ufos-and-the-charlie-red-star-sightings/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: darkpoutine.com">[Dark Poutine]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">darkpoutine.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Dark PoutineMore on Canadian UFOs and The Charlie Red Star...16 Oct 2023 — In the show&#x27;s second half, we learn about a series of UFO sig...</span></span></span>

The Diemerts’ initial report occurred at the very beginning of this sequence.

## The April 10, 1975 Encounter

According to contemporary reporting and later summaries derived from witness interviews, Bob and Elaine Diemert were walking from their farmhouse toward their private airstrip near Carman on the evening of April 10, 1975. During that walk they noticed an unusual red light approaching from the west. The object was not described merely as a distant star-like point. Instead, the witnesses reported a substantial glowing object with a prominent red appearance and a distinctive pulsing quality.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://mbnews101.ca/carman-dufferin-standard/fifty-years-have-passed-since-charlie-red-star-phenomenon-in-1975/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: mbnews101.ca">[Manitoba News 101]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">mbnews101.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Manitoba News 101Fifty years have passed since Charlie Red Star...March 20, 2025 — 20 Mar 2025 — On April 10, 1975, Bob and Elaine Dieme...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 20, 2025</span></span></span>

A detailed account preserved in historical Manitoba UFO documentation states that the object appeared saucer-shaped and was illuminated by a long glowing pulsating red line. As it moved eastward toward Carman, the object reportedly traveled at relatively low altitude and remained visible for several minutes. Bob Diemert estimated that it flew roughly 300 feet above the treetops at approximately 30 miles per hour (about 50 kilometers per hour). The witnesses observed it for roughly five to seven minutes as it crossed the landscape before disappearing beyond the tree line. Notably, they reported no accompanying sound.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://files.bluebookfiles.org/pdfs/17540.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: files.bluebookfiles.org">[Bluebook Files]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">files.bluebookfiles.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Qe T&#x27;s down in Carman right now checking into all these UFO stories--.Read more</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Bluebook FilesManitoba UFO Stuff - Project Blue Book ArchiveFebruary 16, 2026 — 20 Aug 1975 — Bob and Elaine Diemert were out for a walk...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 16, 2026</span></span></span>

The absence of noise became one of the more intriguing aspects of the report. Conventional aircraft operating at such low altitude would normally be expected to generate audible engine sounds. Whether the Diemerts accurately judged the object’s distance and altitude remains debatable, but their perception of a low, slow, silent red object would be echoed in later Charlie Red Star reports.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://files.bluebookfiles.org/pdfs/17540.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: files.bluebookfiles.org">[Bluebook Files]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">files.bluebookfiles.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Qe T&#x27;s down in Carman right now checking into all these UFO stories--.Read more</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Bluebook FilesManitoba UFO Stuff - Project Blue Book ArchiveFebruary 16, 2026 — 20 Aug 1975 — Bob and Elaine Diemert were out for a walk...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 16, 2026</span></span></span>

## What the Witnesses Claimed to See

The significance of the Diemert sighting lies not only in its timing but also in the specific descriptive details that later became hallmarks of Charlie Red Star.

Several recurring features appeared in the first report:

* A bright red coloration rather than a white or multicolored light.
* A pulsing or glowing appearance.
* Apparent low-altitude movement near treetop level.
* Slow horizontal travel.
* Abrupt directional change, including a reported veer toward the north.
* No obvious engine noise.
* Visibility lasting several minutes rather than a brief flash or transient event.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://mbnews101.ca/carman-dufferin-standard/fifty-years-have-passed-since-charlie-red-star-phenomenon-in-1975/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: mbnews101.ca">[Manitoba News 101+2Bluebook Files]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">mbnews101.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Manitoba News 101Fifty years have passed since Charlie Red Star...March 20, 2025 — 20 Mar 2025 — On April 10, 1975, Bob and Elaine Dieme...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 20, 2025</span></span></span>

These characteristics mattered because they helped create a recognizable pattern. When later witnesses reported seeing a similar red glowing object exhibiting similar behavior, observers increasingly believed they were witnessing the same phenomenon.

The nickname “Charlie Red Star” itself emerged later, after repeated sightings had turned the mysterious red object into a local celebrity. Yet many of the traits associated with Charlie Red Star can already be found in the Diemerts’ first account.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://darkpoutine.com/2023/10/289-spooktober-3-more-on-canadian-ufos-and-the-charlie-red-star-sightings/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: darkpoutine.com">[Dark Poutine]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">darkpoutine.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Dark PoutineMore on Canadian UFOs and The Charlie Red Star...16 Oct 2023 — In the show&#x27;s second half, we learn about a series of UFO sig...</span></span></span>

## Corroboration and Context

The Diemert report was not entirely isolated. Historical records indicate that another local resident, Mrs. C. Pauls of Carman, recalled seeing an unexplained flash of light around the same period, although she did not directly observe the object itself. While this does not constitute strong independent confirmation, it demonstrates that unusual visual phenomena were being noticed by multiple individuals in the area.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://files.bluebookfiles.org/pdfs/17540.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: files.bluebookfiles.org">[Bluebook Files]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">files.bluebookfiles.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Qe T&#x27;s down in Carman right now checking into all these UFO stories--.Read more</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Bluebook FilesManitoba UFO Stuff - Project Blue Book ArchiveFebruary 16, 2026 — 20 Aug 1975 — Bob and Elaine Diemert were out for a walk...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 16, 2026</span></span></span>

Researchers studying UFO reports often place considerable weight on whether witnesses had prior expectations or motivations. In the case of the April 10 sighting, there is little evidence that the Diemerts were attempting to generate publicity before the Charlie Red Star story became widely known. Their report preceded the massive public interest that would later develop around Carman.

That timing is important because it reduces, though does not eliminate, the possibility that the witnesses were influenced by an already-established local legend. At the moment of their observation, no Charlie Red Star folklore yet existed.

## From Individual Observation to Regional Phenomenon

The Diemerts continued reporting unusual aerial objects after the first encounter. According to later historical summaries, sightings intensified dramatically beginning in early May 1975. By May 7, recurring observations had become common enough that people started gathering near the Diemerts’ property specifically to watch the skies.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://mbnews101.ca/carman-dufferin-standard/fifty-years-have-passed-since-charlie-red-star-phenomenon-in-1975/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: mbnews101.ca">[Manitoba News 101]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">mbnews101.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Manitoba News 101Fifty years have passed since Charlie Red Star...March 20, 2025 — 20 Mar 2025 — On April 10, 1975, Bob and Elaine Dieme...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 20, 2025</span></span></span>

These gatherings eventually grew into informal UFO-watching events. Visitors traveled from other parts of Manitoba, elsewhere in Canada, and even the United States hoping to witness the mysterious red object. Newspaper coverage amplified public interest. Local businesses capitalized on the phenomenon, and Charlie Red Star rapidly entered regional folklore.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://mbnews101.ca/carman-dufferin-standard/fifty-years-have-passed-since-charlie-red-star-phenomenon-in-1975/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: mbnews101.ca">[Manitoba News 101]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">mbnews101.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Manitoba News 101Fifty years have passed since Charlie Red Star...March 20, 2025 — 20 Mar 2025 — On April 10, 1975, Bob and Elaine Dieme...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 20, 2025</span></span></span>

The Diemerts’ original sighting therefore functioned as the catalyst for a much larger social event. Without that first report, the later wave might never have acquired a coherent identity.

## Evaluating the Reliability of the Report

The first Carman report presents both strengths and weaknesses from an evidentiary standpoint.

On the positive side, the observation reportedly lasted several minutes rather than a few seconds. Extended observation generally provides witnesses more opportunity to judge movement and appearance.
