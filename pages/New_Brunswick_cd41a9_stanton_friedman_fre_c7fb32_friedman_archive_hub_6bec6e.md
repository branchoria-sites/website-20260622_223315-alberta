## Introduction

Fredericton became a notable destination for UFO researchers not because of a famous local sighting, but because it became the home of one of the largest privately assembled UFO research collections in Canada. In the years before his death in 2019, longtime Fredericton resident Stanton [Friedman]({{ 'friedman/' | relative_url }}) arranged for his research files to be preserved by the Provincial Archives of [New Brunswick]({{ 'what-really-happened-in-new-brunswick/' | relative_url }}) (PANB). The transfer transformed the city from the residence of a well-known UFO advocate into a practical archival centre where decades of correspondence, case files, publications, photographs, recordings and investigative material could be preserved and studied.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.vice.com/en/article/the-largest-single-collection-of-ufo-material-is-being-cataloged/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: vice.com">[VICE]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">vice.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Largest Single Collection of UFO Material Is Being</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Largest Single Collection of UFO Material Is Being...October 29, 2019 — 29 Oct 2019 — Archivists are actively cataloging what is...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 29, 2019</span></span></span>

For New Brunswick's UFO history, this matters because the province gained something rare: a professionally managed archive devoted to a subject that has often been documented through scattered private collections. At the same time, the existence of a large archive does not validate the claims found within it. The collection's importance lies in preservation, access and historical documentation rather than proof of extraterrestrial visitation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://activehistory.ca/blog/2020/05/12/archivists-in-isolation/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: activehistory.ca">[Active History]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">activehistory.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Active History Archivists in Isolation</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Active HistoryArchivists in Isolation - Active HistoryMay 12, 2020 — 12 May 2020 — Joanna Aiton Kerr, Private Records Manager – Provincia...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 12, 2020</span></span></span>

## What the Friedman collection contains

Friedman's archive reflects more than fifty years of involvement in UFO research, public advocacy and investigation. Archivists described receiving an enormous and highly varied body of material accumulated during decades of lectures, correspondence and case work. The collection reportedly included letters from witnesses, exchanges with other researchers, speaking schedules, photographs, audio and video recordings, research notes, government information requests, draft manuscripts and a wide range of UFO-related artefacts.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://activehistory.ca/blog/2020/05/12/archivists-in-isolation/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: activehistory.ca">[Active History]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">activehistory.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Active History Archivists in Isolation</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Active HistoryArchivists in Isolation - Active HistoryMay 12, 2020 — 12 May 2020 — Joanna Aiton Kerr, Private Records Manager – Provincia...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 12, 2020</span></span></span>

The diversity of the material is one reason the collection attracted attention beyond the UFO community. Rather than preserving only Friedman's published books or lectures, the archive captures the working process behind his advocacy. Researchers can potentially trace how particular claims developed, how information moved through UFO networks, and how debates unfolded between believers, sceptics and investigators.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://activehistory.ca/blog/2020/05/12/archivists-in-isolation/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: activehistory.ca">[Active History]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">activehistory.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Active History Archivists in Isolation</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Active HistoryArchivists in Isolation - Active HistoryMay 12, 2020 — 12 May 2020 — Joanna Aiton Kerr, Private Records Manager – Provincia...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 12, 2020</span></span></span>

The collection also preserves records connected to some of the most influential stories in modern UFO culture, including Roswell-related research and other cases that Friedman promoted throughout his career. Because he corresponded internationally, the archive extends far beyond New Brunswick itself, linking Fredericton to a much wider North American and international research network.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.vice.com/en/article/the-largest-single-collection-of-ufo-material-is-being-cataloged/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: vice.com">[VICE]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">vice.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Largest Single Collection of UFO Material Is Being</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Largest Single Collection of UFO Material Is Being...October 29, 2019 — 29 Oct 2019 — Archivists are actively cataloging what is...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 29, 2019</span></span></span>

## How the Provincial Archives received and processed it

The practical reason Fredericton became a UFO archive hub is that the collection was not simply donated and boxed away. The Provincial Archives of New Brunswick actively acquired it and committed professional resources to preserving it. Archivists learned that Friedman was retiring from active UFO research and worked to secure the records for permanent preservation in New Brunswick.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.vice.com/en/article/the-largest-single-collection-of-ufo-material-is-being-cataloged/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: vice.com">[VICE]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">vice.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Largest Single Collection of UFO Material Is Being</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Largest Single Collection of UFO Material Is Being...October 29, 2019 — 29 Oct 2019 — Archivists are actively cataloging what is...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 29, 2019</span></span></span>

The scale of the transfer was remarkable. Archivists reported that the acquisition filled five cargo vans and occupied multiple rooms during processing. The records arrived in a condition described as highly disorganised, creating a substantial cataloguing challenge. Archivists estimated that fully arranging, describing and preparing the material for researchers would require years of work because of both the collection's size and complexity.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.leonarddavid.com/ufo-research-the-stanton-friedman-collection/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: leonarddavid.com">[Leonard David+2Active History]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">leonarddavid.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ufo research the stanton friedman collection</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Leonard DavidUFO Research: The Stanton Friedman Collection12 Nov 2019 — Stanton Friedman donated his invaluable research papers to the ar...</span></span></span>

This professional processing distinguishes the Friedman papers from many private UFO collections. Archives do not simply store records; they organise them, preserve their provenance, document relationships between files and create finding aids that allow future researchers to understand context. Without that work, large collections can become effectively unusable. The PANB project therefore turned Fredericton into a place where researchers could expect archival standards rather than informal storage.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://activehistory.ca/blog/2020/05/12/archivists-in-isolation/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: activehistory.ca">[Active History]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">activehistory.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Active History Archivists in Isolation</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Active HistoryArchivists in Isolation - Active HistoryMay 12, 2020 — 12 May 2020 — Joanna Aiton Kerr, Private Records Manager – Provincia...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 12, 2020</span></span></span>

## Why researchers began looking to Fredericton

The archive's significance comes partly from concentration. UFO-related records are often dispersed among private collectors, organisations, retired investigators and family estates. By contrast, the Friedman papers brought a vast quantity of material into a single institutional repository. Contemporary reporting described the collection as potentially the largest single body of UFO material ever handled by professional archivists.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.vice.com/en/article/the-largest-single-collection-of-ufo-material-is-being-cataloged/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: vice.com">[VICE]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">vice.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Largest Single Collection of UFO Material Is Being</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Largest Single Collection of UFO Material Is Being...October 29, 2019 — 29 Oct 2019 — Archivists are actively cataloging what is...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 29, 2019</span></span></span>

That concentration created a new reason for researchers to pay attention to Fredericton. Historians of UFO culture, journalists, sociologists, folklorists and aviation researchers gained a potential access point to decades of documentation that previously sat in a private residence. The archive therefore expanded New Brunswick's role from hosting a prominent UFO advocate to preserving a substantial portion of the documentary history surrounding modern UFO debates.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.leonarddavid.com/ufo-research-the-stanton-friedman-collection/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: leonarddavid.com">[Leonard David]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">leonarddavid.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ufo research the stanton friedman collection</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Leonard DavidUFO Research: The Stanton Friedman Collection12 Nov 2019 — Stanton Friedman donated his invaluable research papers to the ar...</span></span></span>

The collection also influenced local public history. The Fredericton Region Museum developed exhibits using family-donated materials and archival documents connected to the broader collection. Those displays helped introduce the archive's existence to visitors who might otherwise have known little about either Friedman or New Brunswick's place in UFO history.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.frederictonregionmuseum.com/2021/06/16/stanton-friedman-is-out-of-this-world-exhibit-opens-june-26/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: frederictonregionmuseum.com">[Fredericton Region Museum+2Fredericton Region Museum]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">frederictonregionmuseum.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Fredericton Region Museum Stanton Friedman Is Out Of This World</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Fredericton Region MuseumStanton Friedman Is Out Of This WorldJune 16, 2021 — 30 Jun 2022 — It also contains never before seen archival d...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 16, 2021</span></span></span>

## What preservation does and does not prove

A common misunderstanding is that an archive's existence somehow validates the claims contained within it. Archival preservation does not work that way. The Provincial Archives of New Brunswick preserves records because they have historical, cultural or research value, not because archivists endorse the conclusions found in those records.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://activehistory.ca/blog/2020/05/12/archivists-in-isolation/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: activehistory.ca">[Active History]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">activehistory.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Active History Archivists in Isolation</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Active HistoryArchivists in Isolation - Active HistoryMay 12, 2020 — 12 May 2020 — Joanna Aiton Kerr, Private Records Manager – Provincia...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 12, 2020</span></span></span>

For UFO researchers, the Friedman papers are valuable because they preserve evidence about investigations, arguments, correspondence networks and public advocacy. Verification still requires examining original sources, corroborating evidence, competing explanations and later scholarship.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://activehistory.ca/blog/2020/05/12/archivists-in-isolation/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: activehistory.ca">[Active History]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">activehistory.ca</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Active History Archivists in Isolation</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Active HistoryArchivists in Isolation - Active HistoryMay 12, 2020 — 12 May 2020 — Joanna Aiton Kerr, Private Records Manager – Provincia...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 12, 2020</span></span></span>

Most discussions of UFO history in New Brunswick focus on sightings, witness reports or unexplained aerial events. The Friedman collection represents a different kind of historical asset.
